GTA RP - Episodes

GTA RP - Episodes is a private tool used by a single creator to edit and publish videos on their own YouTube channels (GTA RP episodes and League of Legends streamer reaction compilations).

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the creator's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read their view statistics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
