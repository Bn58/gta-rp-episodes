Privacy Policy — GTA RP - Episodes

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What the app accesses

With the account owner's consent (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services in order to upload videos, set their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist, privacy status) and read basic statistics such as views.

How data is stored

The OAuth token and the data read from YouTube are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Who uses it

The app is used only by its owner, for their own channels. It does not collect data from other users or viewers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
